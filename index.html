Our story began in Shimla in 1934 when Rai Bahadur M.S. Oberoi took the helm of his first hotel and thus pioneered luxury hospitality in India. The lessons were self-taught, wisdom was gained and international acclaim soon followed.To this day, we practice the heartfelt operational philosophies that were first defined by Rai Bahadur M.S. Oberoi, carried forward by his son Mr. P.R. S. Oberoi and cherished by his grandsons Mr. Arjun Oberoi, MD and Mr. Vikram Oberoi, CEO - the third generation of Oberoi leaders: The guest is everything. This philosophy is evident in our people and manifested most visibly in the sincere personal care and attention that is afforded to every guest.

About Resort

A Timeless Experience of Luxury, Elegance and Indian Heritage

The The Oberoi Amarvilas is one of India's most exceptional luxury hotels, offering an unforgettable hospitality experience inspired by the beauty, history and architectural grandeur of the Mughal era. Located just a short distance from the magnificent Taj Mahal in Agra, the hotel provides guests with spectacular views of this world-famous monument from every room and suite. Designed to reflect the elegance of traditional Mughal architecture, the property features beautiful landscaped gardens, elegant fountains, decorative courtyards, peaceful water channels and magnificent interiors. Every corner of the hotel showcases a perfect combination of traditional Indian craftsmanship and contemporary luxury, creating an atmosphere of sophistication, comfort and tranquillity. The hotel offers a collection of luxurious accommodation options, including Premier Rooms, Premier Rooms with Balcony, Deluxe Suites, Deluxe Suites with Balcony, Luxury Suites and the exclusive Kohinoor Suite. Each accommodation category is thoughtfully designed with elegant furnishings, premium bedding, spacious interiors and beautifully appointed marble bathrooms. Guests can enjoy breathtaking Taj Mahal views while experiencing exceptional comfort and personalised hospitality. Dining at The Oberoi Amarvilas is an experience in itself. The hotel offers exquisite culinary experiences featuring authentic Indian specialities and international cuisine. Guests can enjoy carefully prepared dishes in elegant dining spaces while experiencing warm hospitality and impressive surroundings. The restaurants combine traditional flavours, premium ingredients and sophisticated presentation to create memorable dining experiences. For guests seeking relaxation and rejuvenation, the hotel provides luxurious spa and wellness facilities, including relaxing treatments, massages and wellness experiences. The swimming pool, fitness facilities and peaceful outdoor surroundings offer additional opportunities to unwind and enjoy a refreshing holiday. The property also provides exceptional spaces for special occasions, romantic celebrations, private dining experiences and memorable gatherings. Its beautiful architecture, luxurious atmosphere and remarkable location make it an ideal destination for couples, families, international travellers and guests looking for an extraordinary luxury getaway. What truly makes The Oberoi Amarvilas special is its commitment to delivering personalised service and outstanding attention to detail. From the moment guests arrive, they are welcomed with genuine Indian hospitality, attentive staff and thoughtfully designed experiences that make every stay memorable. Whether enjoying the breathtaking beauty of the Taj Mahal, relaxing in a luxurious suite, exploring the Mughal-inspired gardens or experiencing exceptional dining, every moment at The Oberoi Amarvilas reflects elegance, exclusivity and timeless luxury.

Resort Facilities

GYM

Maintain your fitness routine and embrace a healthy lifestyle at our state-of-the-art Fitness Centre, thoughtfully designed to provide an exceptional workout experience in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Whether you prefer an energetic morning workout, strength training or a relaxing exercise session, our modern gym offers the perfect setting to keep your body active and your mind refreshed throughout your stay.The fitness centre features a wide selection of modern exercise equipment, including advanced treadmills, elliptical cross trainers, stationary bicycles, rowing machines, free weights and strength-training equipment. Every facility is carefully arranged to provide guests with a convenient and enjoyable fitness experience, whether they are beginners or experienced fitness enthusiasts. Designed with spacious interiors, elegant surroundings and excellent ventilation, the gym creates a refreshing atmosphere where guests can focus on their personal fitness goals. Large windows allow natural light to enter the space, adding to the peaceful and energising ambience. Guests can enjoy cardiovascular workouts, muscle-strengthening exercises, flexibility training and other physical activities according to their individual preferences.

Swiming pool

Pure relaxation and luxury at our beautifully designed resort swimming pool, where crystal-clear waters meet breathtaking surroundings. Whether you want to enjoy a refreshing morning swim, relax under the warm sunshine, or spend quality time with family and friends, our swimming pool offers the perfect escape from everyday life. Surrounded by comfortable sun loungers, elegant landscaping, and a peaceful atmosphere, it creates an ideal setting for both relaxation and recreation. Guests can enjoy a refreshing dip, unwind beside the pool with their favourite beverages, or simply admire the stunning views while experiencing the comfort of a luxury resort. With a clean and well-maintained swimming area, dedicated relaxation spaces, and a welcoming ambience, our pool promises an unforgettable experience for guests of all ages.

Kids park

little ones discover a world of fun, adventure, and excitement at our beautifully designed kids' park.
