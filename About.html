Our story

Our story began in Shimla in 1934 when Rai Bahadur M.S. Oberoi took the helm of his first hotel and thus pioneered luxury hospitality in India. The lessons were self-taught, wisdom was gained and international acclaim soon followed.

To this day, we practice the heartfelt operational philosophies that were first defined by Rai Bahadur M.S. Oberoi, carried forward by his son Mr. P.R. S. Oberoi and cherished by his grandsons Mr. Arjun Oberoi, MD and Mr. Vikram Oberoi, CEO - the third generation of Oberoi leaders: The guest is everything. This philosophy is evident in our people and manifested most visibly in the sincere personal care and attention that is afforded to every guest.

While it is true that our exceptional properties, accommodations and facilities contribute greatly to the esteem in which we are held, we measure our success by the value of the human interaction practiced by our people and deeply felt by our guests.

The Group

The Oberoi Group currently operates 32 hotels and two River Nile Cruisers. The Group has presence in seven countries under the luxury ‘Oberoi’ and five-star ‘Trident’ brands. It is also engaged in flight catering, airport restaurants, car rentals and corporate air charters.

Our Commitment

The Group’s commitment to excellence, attention to detail and personalised service has ensured a loyal list of guests and global accolades.

Recognising the importance of quality training in hospitality management, The Oberoi Group established The Oberoi Centre of Learning and Development in New Delhi in 1966.

The Oberoi Group is also committed to employing the best environmental and ecological practices.
