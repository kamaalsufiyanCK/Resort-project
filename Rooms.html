Our story began in Shimla in 1934 when Rai Bahadur M.S. Oberoi took the helm of his first hotel and thus pioneered luxury hospitality in India. The lessons were self-taught, wisdom was gained and international acclaim soon followed.To this day, we practice the heartfelt operational philosophies that were first defined by Rai Bahadur M.S. Oberoi, carried forward by his son Mr. P.R. S. Oberoi and cherished by his grandsons Mr. Arjun Oberoi, MD and Mr. Vikram Oberoi, CEO - the third generation of Oberoi leaders: The guest is everything. This philosophy is evident in our people and manifested most visibly in the sincere personal care and attention that is afforded to every guest.

Premium Room

A premier experience of Oberoi hospitality.

Uninterrupted garden views are best enjoyed from the cushioned bay window seat in these stunning rooms with 55.74 square metres interiors. Decorated in a natural palette, with vibrant touches of colour and traditional Indian patterns, the spacious interiors include a king sized bed and a marble en-suite bathroom with views of a private sun dappled courtyard.

Large bay windows feature a comfortable cushioned seating area where guests can sit back and enjoy uninterrupted views of the lush green gardens. The interiors are decorated with a soothing natural colour palette, complemented by vibrant colour accents and traditional Indian patterns that create a warm, welcoming and sophisticated atmosphere
