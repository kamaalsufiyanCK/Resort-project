Our story began in Shimla in 1934 when Rai Bahadur M.S. Oberoi took the helm of his first hotel and thus pioneered luxury hospitality in India. The lessons were self-taught, wisdom was gained and international acclaim soon followed.To this day, we practice the heartfelt operational philosophies that were first defined by Rai Bahadur M.S. Oberoi, carried forward by his son Mr. P.R. S. Oberoi and cherished by his grandsons Mr. Arjun Oberoi, MD and Mr. Vikram Oberoi, CEO - the third generation of Oberoi leaders: The guest is everything. This philosophy is evident in our people and manifested most visibly in the sincere personal care and attention that is afforded to every guest.
